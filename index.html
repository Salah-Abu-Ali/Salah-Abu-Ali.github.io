01 // Background

The Bridge Between Equations & Play

Mathematics is not merely abstract symbol manipulation—it is the underlying engine of physics, animation, and dynamic game mechanics.

As both a game developer and a mathematics educator, I approach game design from first principles. Whether implementing spatial partitioning algorithms, writing custom physics solvers, or tuning procedural curve trajectories, I look for the simplest and most elegant mathematical model to produce responsive, organic gameplay.

Game Engineering

Crafting robust mechanics, deterministic simulations, puzzle rule engines, and performant C#/C++ architecture with clean entity abstractions.

Math Pedagogy

Demystifying linear algebra, trigonometry, vectors, and calculus through visual explanations, tactile playgrounds, and intuitive analogies.

Core Principles

  • — Determinism & Clarity: Code and mathematical mechanics should behave predictably without hidden magic numbers.
  • — Juice from Physics: Good game feel arises from natural acceleration, damping curves, and harmonic springs.
  • — Playful Learning: Learners grasp complex theorems faster when they can tweak variables in real time.
