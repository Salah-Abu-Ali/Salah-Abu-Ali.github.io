My Story & Background

How an autodidact background shaped my work.

I started my journey with computer graphics back in 2010 at age 12. Without an internet connection or video tutorials, I learned 3ds Max cover-to-cover from printed books. That experience gave me an early, hands-on spatial foundation in 3D geometry, polygon topology, and coordinate transformations.

In 2018, I began making games professionally. I entered my first game dev competition—The Very Big Indie Pitch (Pocket Gamer) at the Jordan Gaming Summit—and placed 3rd with my first prototype. That confirmed to me that building interactive systems was my path.

In January 2022, I graduated with a Bachelor's Degree in Mathematics. While I have taught math privately, I realized that math is most exciting when written into code—powering game mechanics, procedural algorithms, physics engines, and visual simulations.

I am a Palestinian refugee living in Amman, Jordan. Because of legal restrictions on work, healthcare, and formal employment, traditional career doors are often closed. Rather than letting that stop me, it pushed me to become an uncompromising self-learner—building commercial games, mastering industrial laser machines, and creating educational math content online.
