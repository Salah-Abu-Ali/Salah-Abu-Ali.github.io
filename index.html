PuzzleMe: A Fractal-Based Jigsaw Universe

PuzzleMe represents the ultimate synthesis of my skills: advanced recursive mathematics, custom Unity engine programming, and physical industrial laser craftsmanship.

DIGITAL GAME (UNITY / C#) Available on Android

Mathematical Fractal Jigsaw Engine

Instead of standard interlocking tabs, PuzzleMe generates jigsaw puzzle pieces using recursive fractal curves and non-trivial geometric subdivision. Every piece behaves as a unique topological tile, requiring a custom deterministic state engine, procedural mesh generation, and optimized 2D spatial partitioning to maintain 60 FPS on mobile hardware.

02 // The Journey

Forged Through Curiosity & Resilience

A timeline of self-taught engineering, academic mathematics, and creating against systemic barriers.

Amman, Jordan

As a Palestinian refugee living in Jordan, legal and employment rights are heavily restricted. Rather than seeing this as a dead end, it pushed me to become an uncompromising autodidact—building real commercial products, publishing games, and sharing mathematical knowledge freely.

2010 • AGE 12

Learning 3ds Max from Physical Books

Without broadband internet or online courses, I taught myself 3ds Max and 3D computer graphics cover-to-cover from printed books. This early start forged a deep spatial intuition for geometry, topology, UV unwrapping, lighting, and coordinate spaces that underpins all my work today.

2018 • BREAKTHROUGH

3rd Place at The Very Big Indie Pitch (Pocket Gamer)

Entered professional game development and competed in my very first game dev contest at the Jordan Gaming Summit 2018. Competing against experienced regional studios, my prototype took 3rd Place at Pocket Gamer's acclaimed Big Indie Pitch, marking the official beginning of my professional game development career.

JANUARY 2022

B.Sc. in Mathematics

Graduated with a Bachelor's Degree in Mathematics. While I teach math privately, I quickly discovered that mathematics achieves its greatest visual power inside game engines and code—transforming abstract linear algebra, differential calculus, and topology into responsive physical systems.

2022 – PRESENT

Math Educator @mathpisalah & Commercial Unity Assets

Launched content channels under @mathpisalah on YouTube and Instagram to make mathematical concepts visual and intuitive. Developed commercial Unity assets for developers on the Unity Asset Store, and brought mathematics into the physical world through industrial CO2 laser engineering.
